Privacy

Draft for ERD review. This page describes the app as currently built in the v1 outline. Update before any public release.

Tableboard TV is designed for local play.

  • Play happens on your local network between Apple TV and nearby iPhones (Bonjour / local networking).
  • There are no accounts to create and no cloud login in the app as built.
  • The app as built does not collect personal data for analytics, advertising, or a remote backend.
  • Player names and colors you enter stay on the devices participating in that table session.

This website is separate from the app. If you email support@tableboardtv.com, we receive whatever you include in that message so we can respond.

Questions about this draft: support@tableboardtv.com.
