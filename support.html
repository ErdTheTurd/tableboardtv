FAQ

How do players join?

Start a table on Apple TV. A four-letter room code appears. On each iPhone, open the companion on the same Wi‑Fi, enter that code, and pick a name and color.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in. Names and colors exist only for that table session on the devices in the room.

Why can’t a phone find the table?

Phones and the Apple TV must be on the same local Wi‑Fi. Tableboard uses Bonjour local networking; guest networks and some AP isolation settings block discovery.

What is Demo Table?

On the TV alone, Demo Table seats bot players so you can try Eights without phones. Real friends still join through a hosted table and the room code.

How do you play Eights?

Match the discard’s suit or rank. Eights are wild and let you name the next suit. First player to empty their hand wins. Two to six players.

Where are my cards shown?

Only on your phone. The TV shows the shared felt — discard, draw pile count, and public seat info — not other players’ hands.
