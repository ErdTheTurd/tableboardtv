How a night works

One Apple TV hosts. Every phone is a private hand. Everyone stays on the local network.

  1. 1

    Host on Apple TV

    Open Tableboard TV and start a table. A four-letter room code appears on the screen.

  2. 2

    Join from each iPhone

    On the same Wi‑Fi, open the companion, enter the room code, pick a name and color. No sign-in.

  3. 3

    Play the shared table

    The TV shows the felt, discard, and whose turn it is. Each phone shows only that player’s cards.
