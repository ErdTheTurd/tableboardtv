Board night on the big screen. Each player keeps a private hand on their iPhone, connected over your local network.

See how it works

What it is

Tableboard TV hosts the shared table on Apple TV. Nearby phones join as companions so everyone can see the game—and no one else can see your cards.

How it works

Three steps from empty living room to a full table.

  1. 1

    Host on Apple TV

    Start a table. A short room code appears on the screen.

  2. 2

    Join on iPhone

    Phones find the table on your local network and keep each hand private.

  3. 3

    Play together

    The TV shows the shared table. Phones show only your cards.

Eights

The first game is Eights—the public-domain shedding classic often called Crazy Eights. Match suit or rank; eights are wild.

More original and public-domain tables are planned. Trademarked game names are not used.
